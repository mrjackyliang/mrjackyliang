Privacy Policy
==============

_Effective November 20, 2026_

This policy covers the open-source projects that Jacky Liang ("I", "me", "my") publishes under the [mrjackyliang](https://github.com/mrjackyliang) GitHub account, their documentation sites, and their issue trackers. It explains what information is involved when you use them and who handles it.

## The Software

My projects are self-hosted. You install and run them on your own devices, servers, and accounts.

Some projects include telemetry that sends usage or diagnostic information, such as versions, errors, or how features are used, so I can understand how the projects are used and improve them. A project may or may not offer a setting to turn this off. You are free to block it with a DNS blocker, a firewall, or similar tools.

Some projects connect to third-party services that you choose to use with them, such as a security system provider, Discord, GitHub, a VPN provider, or your firewall. Those connections go directly between your installation and that service, and that service's own privacy policy applies to them.

## Documentation Sites

The documentation sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The documentation sites use Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the pages are visited, such as which pages are read, roughly where visitors come from, and how visitors scroll and click, including heatmaps and session recordings. This information is used only to improve the documentation. Google handles it under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Microsoft under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement). You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or a content blocker without losing access to the documentation.

## Issues, Discussions, and Pull Requests

Anything you submit in a project's issue tracker, discussions, or pull requests is public on GitHub, including information an issue form asks for, such as an email address, versions, or logs. Remove passwords, tokens, keys, and other personal details before you post. GitHub handles this content under its own terms and privacy statement.

## Sponsorships and Donations

Sponsorships through GitHub Sponsors and donations through PayPal are processed by those services. I receive only what they share with the recipient, such as your name or username and the amount, and I use it only to recognize sponsors. A sponsorship or donation is a voluntary gift, as described in the [Terms of Use](https://github.com/mrjackyliang/mrjackyliang/blob/HEAD/TERMS.md).

## Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy as my projects change. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the relevant project's repository on GitHub.
